# 📈 PUBG Weapons Insights

## 🔍 Overview

This analysis explores weapon performance in PUBG, focusing on damage, DPS, bullet speed, and efficiency.

---

## 📊 Key Metrics

* Total Weapons: **44**
* Maximum Bullet Speed: **990**
* Average Damage: **63.34**
* Average DPS: **1.08K**

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 1. Shotguns Dominate in Raw Damage

Shotguns have the highest average damage among all weapon types, making them extremely effective in close-range combat.

---

### 2. Vector Has the Highest DPS

The Vector stands out with the highest damage per second (DPS), allowing it to deal damage very quickly.

---

### 3. DPS Alone Is Not Enough

Although the Vector has the highest DPS, it is not always the most efficient weapon.

---

### 4. Win94 Is More Efficient in Some Cases

* Requires fewer shots to kill compared to the Vector
* Has higher bullet speed
* More effective for accuracy and long-range scenarios

---

## 🎯 Final Insight

High DPS does not necessarily mean better performance.
Weapon efficiency depends on multiple factors such as bullet speed and shots-to-kill.

---

## 🚀 Conclusion

Choosing the best weapon depends on the situation:

* Close range → Shotguns
* Fast damage → Vector
* Accuracy & efficiency → Win94
